Exceptions

How exceptions are found: for every matched row we calculate TP − ASP = SAP Transfer Price − 2026 ASP ex-Works (rounded to the cent). A row is an exception only when this result is negative by more than $0.01 — meaning the SAP price is lower than the ASP. Rows where the SAP price is higher, equal, or off by only ±$0.01 are ignored. Past Due = report date − the SAP price’s Valid From date (how many days the price has been in effect at the wrong value); exceptions are sorted longest-outstanding first. Each market gets its own exceptions file, with its own remembered recipient. The always send to addresses are appended to every exceptions email.
