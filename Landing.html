First time here?

Each tool reads its raw data from a Google Sheet. You can point them all at the same sheet, or give each tool its own. Click the button below (or the ⚙ Settings button at the top of this page, or the ⚙ Data sheet button at the top of each tool) and paste your sheet link(s). You can change them any time.

QlikView data

Drop the QlikView exports into their Drive folders. Every hour the suite checks whether they have changed, and syncs them if they have — there is nothing to press.

If a page is open when new data lands, it says so and offers a button to load it.
